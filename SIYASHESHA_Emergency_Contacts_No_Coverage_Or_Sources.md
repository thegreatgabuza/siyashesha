SIYASHESHA HEALTH PASSPORT

**South Africa | Emergency & Support Contact Directory**

Prepared 9 November 2026 • App-content and implementation reference

**IMPORTANT: In immediate danger or a life-threatening medical emergency, call 112 from a mobile phone or 10177 for a public ambulance. This directory includes crisis and support lines that are NOT substitutes for emergency dispatch. Call availability and coverage can change.**

| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

# 6. Suggested SIYASHESHA app implementation

- Show 112, 10177 and 10111 as persistent emergency buttons, not mixed with counselling lines.
- Group contacts by purpose: Emergency Response; Medical & Rescue; Mental Health; GBV & Child Protection; Health Information; Regional Contacts.
- Use tap-to-call telephone links. Show location-specific municipal fire and ambulance contacts only where verified.
- Clearly distinguish private emergency services from public emergency lines.
- Add patient-selected next of kin and personal healthcare provider contacts. Permit offline access to essential numbers.
- Before production release, manually re-confirm any line marked for reconfirmation, then schedule periodic number audits.
